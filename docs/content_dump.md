FAQ
Q. Does Gifthappiness take a percentage of the donation or any money?
A. No. We don’t take any fees or charges. The whole amount  is directly credited to the charities account minus the bank/financial/transaction fees.

Q. How are the charities selected? 
A. The charities are selected by a group of individuals associated with gift happiness and have to undergo a strict criteria without exception. 

Q. What are the chances of favouritism or only one charity getting all the money?
A. The criteria includes no relation with any member of Gifthappiness whatsoever. All the charities and the amount received by them are displayed for all to see on the website.
There is a predetermined limit which is the same for all the charities at which they will be removed from the list of beneficiaries/charity. 

Q. Can I suggest my own charity to be featured on my celebration page.
A. Unfortunately not. But for this you don’t need us. You can collect all your contributions and hand it over to them directly. The idea is to gift happiness whether through us or directly.

A. Unfortunately not. Once the amount is debited from your account. It cannot be refunded.
